Experience the most advanced AI chat companion directly on Telegram. Chat with 11+ unique AI characters, generate images, and enjoy multilingual conversations - all in one place!

## ✨ Features

- 🎭 **11+ AI Characters** - From girlfriend to teacher, choose your perfect AI companion
- 🌍 **10+ Languages** - English, 中文, 日本語, Español, Français, and more
- 🎨 **AI Image Generation** - Create stunning images with text descriptions
- 💬 **Smart Conversations** - Context-aware dialogues with memory
- 🔒 **Privacy First** - Your chats stay private and secure
- ⚡ **Lightning Fast** - Powered by DeepSeek AI for instant responses
