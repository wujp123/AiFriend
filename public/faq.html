What is iFriendly AI?
iFriendly AI is an advanced AI chatbot platform built on Telegram. It features 11+ unique AI characters with different personalities, multilingual support for 10+ languages, and AI image generation capabilities. Think of it as having multiple AI companions available 24/7 right in your Telegram app.
How do I start using iFriendly AI?
It's super easy! Just open the Telegram Mini App at t.me/iFriendly_Ai_Bot/ifriendly_app or message @iFriendly_Ai_Bot on Telegram. Your account is automatically created using your Telegram profile. No signup forms, no email verification - just start chatting!
Is iFriendly AI free?
Yes and no. We offer a free tier with limited messages so you can try the platform. For unlimited access to all features, characters, and image generation, you'll need a premium subscription starting at just $2 USDT per week.
Do I need to install anything?
Nope! iFriendly AI runs entirely within Telegram as a Mini App. If you have Telegram installed, you're all set. No separate apps, no downloads, no extra space needed on your device.

🛠️ Technical Support

The app won't load. What should I do?
Try these steps:
  1. Check your internet connection
  2. Close and reopen Telegram
  3. Clear Telegram cache (Settings → Data and Storage → Clear Cache)
  4. Update Telegram to the latest version
  5. Try accessing from a different device
If the problem persists, contact support with details about your device and Telegram version.
The AI is giving weird responses. Why?
This can happen for a few reasons:
  • Your question might be unclear - try rephrasing
  • The AI might lack context - provide more background information
  • The character might not be suitable for your query - try a different character
  • There might be a temporary API issue - wait a minute and retry
Remember, AI works best with clear, specific questions and adequate context.
My premium payment isn't showing up. Help!
Don't worry! Follow these steps:
  1. Wait 1-2 minutes for blockchain confirmation
  2. Check that you used TRON (TRC20) network
  3. Verify you sent to the correct address
  4. Restart the app to refresh your premium status
  5. Contact support with your transaction ID if still not working
99% of payment issues resolve within 5 minutes.

🎓 Advanced Usage

How can I get better responses from the AI?
Pro tips for optimal AI conversations:
  • Be specific: Instead of "help me," try "help me write a professional email to my boss about vacation time"
  • Provide context: Share relevant background information
  • Ask follow-ups: Build on previous responses for deeper conversations
  • Choose the right character: Use Teacher for learning, Psychologist for emotional topics, etc.
  • Use proper grammar: Helps the AI understand your intent better
Can I use iFriendly AI for work or business?
Absolutely! Many users leverage iFriendly AI for professional tasks:
  • Brainstorming ideas with Creative Muse
  • Getting career advice from Career Coach
  • Learning new skills with Teacher
  • Drafting emails and documents
  • Quick research and fact-checking
Premium members have unlimited access perfect for heavy professional use.
What are the best prompts for AI image generation?
Great image prompts include:
  • Subject: What do you want to see?
  • Style: Photorealistic, anime, oil painting, digital art, etc.
  • Details: Colors, lighting, mood, composition
  • Quality modifiers: "highly detailed," "4k," "professional"
Example: "A cozy coffee shop in Tokyo, raining outside, warm lighting, anime style, highly detailed" produces much better results than just "coffee shop."
Can the AI remember information across different characters?
No, each AI character maintains separate conversation memory. This is by design - it allows you to have distinct relationships with different characters. For example, you might discuss work with Career Coach and personal matters with Sister, and they won't cross-reference those conversations.
